IRONCLAD HOME SERVICES – RESPONSIVE WEBSITE V3
==============================================

FILES
  index.html        Full homepage, intentionally spacious and section-based
  landing.html      Shorter launch landing page
  styles.css        Shared responsive styles for both pages
  script.js         Accessible mobile navigation, scroll reveals and inquiry helpers
  assets/           All logo and imagery files, locally hosted

PREVIEW
  Open index.html and landing.html in your browser, or upload the entire folder
  to a static hosting provider. Keep the assets folder alongside the HTML/CSS.

DESIGN CHANGES IN V3
  • Preserved the original house photograph in both page heroes.
  • Replaced the About IronClad image with a collaboration/team scene.
  • Added one handshake photo alongside the process story.
  • Landing page includes a compact human-centered trust section.
  • Restored generous vertical breathing room to the full homepage.
  • Responsive layouts, a mobile menu, keyboard focus, hover states,
    accessible motion reduction, and shared brand styling.
  • New approved metallic IronClad Home Services logo.

IMPORTANT PRE-LAUNCH CHECKS
  • This is a functional FRONT-END mockup, not a production booking portal.
  • Forms open a prewritten email to admin@icladcontracting.com. They do not
    collect/store messages; connect a form backend or WordPress form before
    treating them as live submissions.
  • No payment processor, live calendar, or real-time service booking yet.
  • Logo was approved for the mockup; replace with master brand files if available.
  • Photographs of staff, homeowner handshake, and collaboration are
    illustrative generated visuals, NOT real IronClad employees or clients.
    Replace with authorized actual photography before implying they are staff.
  • Confirm exact service offerings, credentials, coverage, email, and copy
    with the client prior to publication.
  • Fonts are Barlow Condensed + Manrope from Google Fonts; system fallbacks
    are provided when offline.

DEPLOYMENT
  Recommended: upload folder contents to your host (root / public_html),
  or rename landing.html to index.html for a temporary launch (back up
  the full index.html first). Both use the same styles.css and script.js.
