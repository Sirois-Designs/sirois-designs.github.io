A proven local-pass model

A free Passport—and eventually a paid local Pass.

The community Passport rewards participation. A limited annual Viera Local Pass could later offer single-use deals from participating restaurants, shops, wellness businesses, and entertainment partners throughout the district.

Free community layer
✓

Viera Local Passport

Earn digital stamps for joining meetups, exploring nearby places, welcoming neighbors, supporting local businesses, and completing district challenges.

  • Included for residents
  • Participation-based stamps
  • Badges, drawings, and community rewards
  • Apartment and district challenges
Future annual product
VL

Viera Local Pass

A limited annual pass with one-time partner offers designed to encourage residents to try more nearby restaurants, shops, services, and activities.

  • One clear offer per participating location
  • Single-use redemption with simple restrictions
  • Annual validity and limited quantity
  • Direct, trackable value for local partners
