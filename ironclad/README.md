# IRON CLAD CONTRACTING

## Brand Foundation — Version 1

### Brand Idea

**Iron Clad Contracting is a residential renovation and general contracting company built around one simple idea: do the job right, communicate clearly, and deliver work worth standing behind.**

The company should feel strong, dependable, skilled, professional, and premium without becoming cold, corporate, or intimidating.

Iron Clad is not positioned as the cheapest contractor.

It is positioned as the contractor homeowners choose when they want confidence that the project will be handled properly.

# Ideal Customer

Iron Clad is built primarily for homeowners who:

* Own a home they intend to improve rather than simply maintain
* Care about quality and appearance
* Are willing to invest in doing the project properly
* Want a professional contractor rather than the lowest bid
* Value communication
* Want someone they feel comfortable allowing into their home
* Are frustrated by unreliable contractors
* Want guidance throughout the renovation process
* Prefer an organized, professional experience

The customer may not understand construction.

They should not have to.

Iron Clad's job is to make the process understandable and manageable.

---

# Customer Problem

Homeowners frequently enter renovations worried about:

**Will they show up?**

**Will the final price resemble the estimate?**

**Will the project drag on forever?**

**Will they communicate with me?**

**Will they respect my home?**

**Will the finished project actually look good?**

**Will they disappear if something goes wrong?**

Iron Clad should build its marketing around removing these uncertainties.

# Brand Voice

Iron Clad speaks like a knowledgeable contractor who respects the customer's intelligence.

We use:

* Short, confident statements
* Plain English
* Clear explanations
* Strong verbs
* Honest expectations
* Minimal jargon

We avoid:

* Corporate filler
* Construction jargon customers may not understand
* Exaggerated promises
* High-pressure sales language
* Clichés like “your premier one-stop solution”
* Excessive use of words such as luxury, elite, revolutionary, or best-in-class

---

# The Iron Clad Process

The project experience should be branded rather than presented as generic steps.

## 01 — TALK

**Tell us what you're imagining.**

We learn about the home, the problem, the ideas, and what the homeowner hopes to accomplish.

## 02 — PLAN

**Build the roadmap.**

We evaluate the project, establish the scope, and explain what will be involved.

## 03 — BUILD

**Bring the vision to life.**

Construction begins with attention to workmanship, organization, and communication.

## 04 — WALKTHROUGH

**Make sure it's right.**

We review the finished project with the homeowner and address remaining details.

## 05 — ENJOY

**Welcome back to your home.**

The construction is finished. The improved space is yours to enjoy.

---

# Recommended Visual Personality

The Iron Clad aesthetic should combine:

**Modern residential architecture**

with

**premium industrial craftsmanship**

Think:

* Dark navy backgrounds
* Warm white sections
* Gold details
* Large project photography
* Architectural lines
* Subtle blueprint references
* Fine grid patterns
* Steel or brushed-metal textures used sparingly
* Strong rectangular framing
* Clean spacing
* Large typography
* Before-and-after imagery

Avoid turning the brand into stereotypical construction graphics filled with:

* Hard hats
* Hammers
* Wrenches
* Saw blades
* Hazard stripes
* Diamond plate everywhere

The company should look like someone you would trust with a $40,000 kitchen, not a bargain handyman flyer.

# Photography Direction

2. Before-and-after comparisons
3. Detail photography
4. Work-in-progress craftsmanship

Photos should feel bright, clean, architectural, and real.

Whenever possible, use actual Iron Clad projects rather than stock photography.

# Contracting + Academy Relationship

The two companies should play different roles.

### Iron Clad Contracting

**Proof**

It demonstrates that the systems work in a real contracting company.

### Iron Clad Academy

**Process**

It teaches other contractors and aspiring business owners how those systems were created and implemented.

Together:

## **BUILD THE COMPANY. PROVE THE SYSTEM. TEACH THE SYSTEM.**

This should become a foundational strategy for the entire Iron Clad ecosystem.
